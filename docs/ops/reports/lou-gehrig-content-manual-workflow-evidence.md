## Automation implications

### Safe to automate or assist

- metadata completeness checks;
- missing-source detection;
- required status transition validation;
- candidate table formatting;
- evidence report generation.

### Keep human-controlled

- source truth decisions;
- rights approval;
- privacy approval;
- final public-copy approval;
- publication approval.

### Unsafe or deferred

- uncontrolled scraping;
- OCR/enrichment at scale;
- automatic public publication;
- AI-generated factual claims without source verification.
